# Why I Chose Astro for My Digital Archive

Building a portfolio in 2026 often devolves into heavy client-side SPAs that take 4 seconds to hydrate before displaying basic text.

For **PAVAN KUMAR / WORLD**, I needed zero runtime overhead while maintaining full freedom for high-grade GSAP micro-animations.

## The Key Wins with Astro:

1. **Zero JS by Default**: HTML rendered statically at build time.
2. **Framework Agnostic**: Easy integration of UI islands if needed.
3. **Content Collections**: Fully typed Markdown & MDX workflow for long-term SEO.
